Quick start

      
    
        
  1. Charge the battery and place the Crazyflie on flat ground, front (the side with the antenna) away from you.
    2. 
        
  2. Switch the Crazyflie on and wait for the propellers to finish the self-test spin.
    3. 
        
  3. Plug the PS5 controller into this computer with a USB-C cable and press any button. The Controller chip turns green.
    4. 
        
  4. Pick Bluetooth, press Connect and choose your Crazyflie in Chrome's list.
    5. 
        
  5. Choose a flight mode. With a Flow deck use Hover: it's by far the easiest.
    6. 
        
  6. Press △ Triangle to arm, then ✕ Cross to take off (assisted modes), or push the throttle up gently (Manual).
    7. 
        
  7. Press ✕ again to land, then △ to disarm. ○ Circle or Space is the emergency stop.
    8. 
      

      
Want to practise first? Pick Simulator and press Connect. Everything works the same, with no drone at all.

Flight modes

      
    
        
  • Manual (stabilized): the sticks set the tilt angle and the throttle sets the thrust. Works with every Crazyflie. Needs practice.
    • 
        
  • Altitude hold: uses the barometer. Centre the left stick to hold height; up/down climbs or descends. Drifts sideways, so you still steer.
    • 
        
  • Height hold: like altitude hold but uses the downward laser (Flow or Z-ranger deck). Much steadier.
    • 
        
  • Hover (Flow deck): holds height and position. Sticks command speed. Best for beginners.
    • 
        
  • Position hold: world-frame velocity control. Needs Lighthouse, Loco or the Flow deck.
    • 
      

    

    

      
Safety

      
    
        
  • Fly indoors over a plain, textured floor (the Flow deck needs texture), away from people, pets and faces.
    • 
        
  • If the controller unplugs, the tab is hidden or the window loses focus, the app lands automatically in assisted modes and slowly reduces thrust in Manual.
    • 
        
  • If the radio link is lost, the Crazyflie levels itself after 0.5 s and stops its motors after 2 s (firmware watchdog).
    • 
        
  • After an emergency stop the Crazyflie is locked. Switch it off and on again to fly again.
    • 
        
  • Land when the battery warning appears. The controller rumbles on low battery.
    • 
      

    

    

      
Troubleshooting

      
    
        
  • "Bluetooth not available": use Chrome or Edge (not Safari/Firefox) and switch Bluetooth on. School Chromebooks can have Web Bluetooth turned off by the administrator. If double-clicking the file doesn't allow Bluetooth, open the copy hosted on GitHub Pages instead (see the README).
    • 
        
  • Crazyflie not in the Bluetooth list: restart it. If it has talked to a Crazyradio since it was switched on, it has turned Bluetooth off.
    • 
        
  • Controller not detected: press a button after plugging it in. Browsers only reveal gamepads after a button press.
    • 
        
  • Won't take off in Manual: lower the throttle fully once after arming (thrust lock), then push it up.
    • 
        
  • Linux desktop: Chrome hides Web Bluetooth behind chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features. ChromeOS, macOS and Windows need nothing.
    • 
        
  • Crazyradio on Linux: install Bitcraze's udev rules. On Windows: if Chrome can't open it, install the WinUSB driver with Zadig.
    • 
        
  • Drifts in Hover: check the floor has texture and good light, and that the Flow deck chip is green under Decks.
    • 
      

    

    

      
About

      
One HTML file, no dependencies, runs offline. It talks CRTP, the Crazyflie's native protocol, over Bluetooth LE or a Crazyradio. Logging, parameters, console, supervisor arming, emergency stop and the high-level commander all follow the official Bitcraze firmware and libraries.
